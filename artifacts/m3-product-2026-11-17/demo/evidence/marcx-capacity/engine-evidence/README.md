## Limitations

- deterministic repair readiness only: no AI provider and no network is used by this runner.
- false_verified must be 0; a deterministic verified claim rejected by an available independent verifier fails the run.
- when no independent verifier is available, independently_verified is null/not_run and deterministic claims are never relabelled as independently verified.
- host-unavailable or raised repair calls are recorded as not_run with an explicit reason; availability is never fabricated.
- candidate bytes, patch plans, exchanged metadata and independent verifier reports are persisted per sample under samples/<sample_id>/ and covered by SHA256SUMS (evidence only; not executed by the runner).
