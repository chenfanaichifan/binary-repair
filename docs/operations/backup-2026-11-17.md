## 6. Retention and protection recommendation

- 3-2-1 rule: keep the working repository + two independent local backup
  artifacts + at least one offsite copy. Never let the only copy live on the
  same disk as the worktree.
- Keep dated snapshots: at minimum the 2026-11-16 pre-cleanup snapshot (restore
  point for deleted refs) and the post-freeze snapshot for the final delivery.
- Treat the backup as sensitive: it contains the full private history,
  including branches/tags that were deleted from GitHub. Store offsite copies on
  owner-controlled encrypted media (FileVault/BitLocker volume, encrypted disk
  image, or an encrypted archive); do not upload to a public/shared drive.
- Verify before trusting: re-run `verify-offsite-backup.sh` after every transport
  and at least quarterly; rotate/replace media every 12 months; record the
  verification log next to the copy.
- Restore-time budget: mirror clone (~117M) and bundle (~127M) restore in
  seconds on local disk; the drill above took well under one minute.
