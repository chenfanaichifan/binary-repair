## How the defect is observed without a sanitizer

Reading freed memory is usually invisible in stdout: glibc leaves the bytes.
`badSink` asks for a same-size block right after the free, so the tcache LIFO
hands back the just-released block and the stale read sees `'X'` sentinels.
That makes the two behaviours differ by output alone.

This is a technique, not a guarantee — a different allocator or a hardened
build may not reuse the block, and ASan/valgrind wins where available. The
handoff says so, `PROVENANCE.md` says so, and `invalid.json` states the
*repaired* behaviour rather than claiming the unmodified binary always differs.
