## 2. V2 interface — Route A passed, Route B failed by measurement

`probes/v2_interface.py` ran inside the guest on real bytes, comparing each
candidate with the shared original across 16 checks (ELF class, machine, type,
OS/ABI, entry, PT_INTERP, DT_NEEDED, exported dynamic symbols, preservation of
the original PT_LOAD bases, count and permissions of added PT_LOAD segments,
absence of W+X, GNU_STACK, relocation type subset, actual execution, stdout
equality on the valid path).

| route | candidate | checks | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | `c4a159…aa82` | 16/16 | **passed** |
| B | `63fcafa6…6d01` | 15/16 | **failed** — `extra_load_segments_within_contract` |

The Route B failure is concrete: the candidate adds **2** PT_LOAD segments
(`0x4058a8` R-E, `0x407000` R), while `contract.json` `authorized_edits[2]`
allows at most one new R-X PT_LOAD and only as the fallback when the R-X tail
extension is rejected. Its program-header order also changed, which is recorded
as informational rather than as a failure. Raw `readelf -hW -lW -dW -rW
--dyn-syms` dumps for all three binaries are in the package under `readelf/`.

This is a measurement and is open to re-judgement: if the reviewers read
`authorized_edits[2]` differently, the check name and the raw dumps are there to
re-decide, and nothing else in the package depends on the reading. What is not
negotiable is that the two extra segments exist — that is in the raw dump.
