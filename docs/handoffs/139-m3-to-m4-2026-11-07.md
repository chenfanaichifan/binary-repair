Three things worth knowing before you read the output, so it does not read as more
than it is:

1. **The candidate's `unsat` is the empty-disjunction case.** Its `free()` is five
   `nop`, so nothing is freed on that path, and the property question is
   unsatisfiable rather than solved. The evidence says this in `not_claimed`; if
   you quote `unsat=1` as "the solver proved it", that is a misreading and I would
   rather you did not.
2. **The negative control is the original.** Without `known_bad → violated`, a
   `holds` on the candidate means nothing. Check both lines, not just the good one.
3. **`claimed_scope` is `model`.** Not binary-level. A passing V4 does not become
   machine-code proof by being rerun.

If the digest or the sha256 differs, stop and tell me — do not adjust the
expectation. A difference is the finding.
