So this is not "no engine available" — angr is installed. The reason V4 is
not_run is that a real V4 needs a symbolic harness with exact solver
accounting (sat/unsat/unknown/eval per run) and a bound that cannot hide a
timeout behind "proven"; earlier V4 submissions were rejected for concrete
inputs and silent max_steps (docs/handoffs/63, 64, 66, 67). Writing a quick
one here would produce a claim with no accounting behind it, which is worse
than not_run. So: V4 stays not_run, claimed_scope null, no binary-level claim.
