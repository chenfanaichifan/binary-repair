## 6. What was not done, and why it is written down

**The install half did not run.** The guest has no egress in this window: `curl
https://pypi.org/simple/` returns nothing and `ping 192.168.30.1` gets no reply,
even though the host side briefly got its NAT address back. Fifty-four
distributions cannot be installed from an air-gapped guest, so
`provision_angr_venv.sh` ships as a recipe and this record says so rather than
implying a rebuild happened.

The gap is narrow and nameable: a window with egress closes it by running
`VENV=/opt/angr-venv-rebuild sh provision_angr_venv.sh`, then `selfcheck.py`
under that interpreter, then diffing the two freezes. Until that happens, what
is established is "this is the interpreter that produced the evidence and it
still reproduces it", not "this recipe rebuilds that interpreter".

**The guest was reached over IPv6, not IPv4.** At the start of the window
`VMware NAT Service` was stopped, VMnet8 had fallen back to `169.254.29.225`, and
`ping 192.168.30.128` timed out. ARP still resolved the VM MAC, so only routing
was missing, and the guest was reached at `fe80::20c:29ff:fe94:c60f%5` — EUI-64
of `00:0c:29:94:c6:0f`. Starting the service needs elevation this agent does not
have, so IPv4 was left broken instead of being fixed by hand. No measurement
above depends on the transport.

**No gate, and no `verified_by`.** `gate-index.json` in the 124 bundle still
reads V4 `not_run`; `v4-symbolic.json` in the 128 directory is unchanged. An
environment capable of running a producer says nothing about whether the
producer's output passes a gate — that is M4's replay, per `docs/handoffs/62 §1`.
Writing `verified_by="m4"` here would be a self-issued verdict, which is the one
thing an evidence producer must not do. The field is absent by construction:
`selfcheck.py` writes no file, and nothing in this directory carries a
`verified_by` key.

T8.3 stays blocked. One sample, one repair, one observation; no second sample is
described, implied, or scheduled here.
