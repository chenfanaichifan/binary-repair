## 3. Residuals (not failures of the requested checks)

1. **`forbidden_ranges` was relaxed to `[]`.** In `fdaaa7b` the field was
   `Field(min_length=1)`; at `287aebd` it is `list[ForbiddenRange]` with no
   minimum, and the adapter fills `[]` because the harness reports no ranges.
   The property is carried in `memory_predicates`, but the structured
   forbidden-range requirement is weaker. Decide whether the harness must report
   ranges or whether `memory_predicates` is sufficient.
2. **Empty-disjunction is present but not mandatory.** The adapter copies
   `not_claimed`; the validator only requires a “binary-level” exclusion. A
   future producer could omit the empty-disjunction sentence and still convert.
   If it must be mandatory, add a converter check (e.g. when the candidate
   `unsat` has `free_calls=0`, require the phrase).
3. **Calibration requires one failed known-bad, not all.** `known_bad_rejected`
   is `any(...)`, and `failed >= 1`; with a larger suite, one rejected known-bad
   and one accepted known-bad would still pass. The current 2-case suite is
   correct, but the rule is not “all known-bad rejected”.

These do not change the answer to the three requested questions; they are the
next questions if V4 is to be relied on, not just ingested.
